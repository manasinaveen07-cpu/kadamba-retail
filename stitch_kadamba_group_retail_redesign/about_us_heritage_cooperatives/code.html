From the Chairman's Desk format_quote

"A business that only enriches its investors is inadequate for Malenadu. Our enterprise must feed the planter, employ the youth, and preserve soil purity."

When we gathered in Sirsi nine years ago in 2015, conventional retailers treated our region as a mere consumer destination. Our pure Malenadu spices—famed globally for oil density and aroma—were purchased for pittance, blended with fillers in distant metropolises, and sold back to our townsfolk at exorbitant prices.

Kadamba was born to invert this predatory dynamic. By integrating the farmers' collection point with our hypermarket floors and rural credit society, we achieved total vertical accountability. Over these nine continuous fiscal years, every rupee of cooperative surplus has been returned to member dividends, employee welfare, and cold-chain capacity.

We employ over 340 local youth from agrarian families across Sirsi, Siddapur, and Yellapur, providing meaningful retail and supply-chain careers without forcing migration to crowded cities.
