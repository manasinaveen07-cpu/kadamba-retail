## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the sensory richness, trust, and communal vitality of a heritage Malnad and Coastal Karnataka retail enterprise. It bridges the warm, abundant feel of traditional weekly santhes (farmers' markets) with modern, frictionless retail and co-operative services.

### Emotional Response & Philosophy
- **Inviting & Abundant:** Every surface feels stocked, fresh, and generous. It rejects sterile SaaS-style starkness in favor of warmth, culinary vitality, and agricultural authenticity.
- **Rooted & Trustworthy:** Balances the community-grounded ethos of an agricultural cooperative with the crisp precision of a tier-one contemporary supermarket.
- **Expressive Celebration:** Utilizes physical visual metaphors—such as promotional badges reminiscent of painted market signs, festive taglines, and elevated display shelves—without descending into kitsch.

### Design Movement
**Tactile Warm Modernism:** A hybrid of modern retail utility and tactile physical metaphors. Surfaces feature creamy warm foundations, gentle 3D elevations, physical pill badges, and vegetable-dyed color accents rather than synthetic digital pastels.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in agricultural yield, festive spices, and Malnad heritage materials:

- **Primary (`#F36B35` - Tangerine Orange):** Drives immediate conversion, vital promotional anchors, primary action buttons, and loyalty flags. Derived from marigold garlands and ripe Kumta chillies.
- **Secondary (`#69A94B` - Fresh Harvest Green):** Governs farm-fresh produce tags, Ayurvedic purity indicators, dairy seals, and co-operative financial trust markers.
- **Tertiary (`#F6C945` - Sunshine Yellow):** Reserved for flash savings, festive discount rosettes, star ratings, and bundle callouts.
- **Alert / Urgency (`#E95C66` - Coral Red):** Dedicated exclusively to clearance pricing, limited-stock countdowns, expiring daily slots, and cart alert toasts.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#49382C` - Roasted Coffee Brown):** Serves as the primary typographic ink instead of sterile pure black, delivering deep legibility with culinary warmth.
- **Canvas Bases:** Surfaces alternate between Warm Cream (`#FFF7E8`) for page backdrops and clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for product containment cards, ensuring organic separation without harsh dividers.

## Typography

The pairing marries the geometric energy of **Outfit** with the balanced humanist legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers commercial punchiness, festive warmth, and high optical impact for category banners, bundle names, and festival announcements.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Handles product ingredient lists, nutritional specs, co-operative savings disclosures, and transaction confirmations with utmost clarity across both Kannada and English numerical listings.
- **Price Typographic System:** Currency displays always lock the Rupee symbol (`₹`) at equal weight to the integer to ensure no billing ambiguity for elders and mobile-first rural shoppers.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout engineered for dense multi-category catalog navigation and rapid basket building.

- **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout with 16px page margins. Category shelves render as edge-peeking horizontal carousels to showcase catalog depth.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 32px page margins. Employs 2-to-3 column dense item grids.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at 1360px container width with 48px canvas margin, allowing multi-tiered sidebars for departmental browsing (Dairy, Organic Spices, Areca products, Credit Society).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict multiples of 4px. Card internals use compact `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps to retain maximum product visibility above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid the flat, cold look of generic digital catalogs, the elevation system mimics tactile retail packaging and layered merchandising shelves:

- **Canvas Foundation:** `#FFF7E8` (Warm Cream) serves as the base layer, creating an organic counter-top feel.
- **Level 1 (Shelf Rest):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.04)`. Used for default product tiles, store notices, and list rows against the cream backdrop.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Engagement):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.12), 0 3px 6px -1px rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.06)`. Applied when an item card is focused or hovered, lifting the card slightly (-2px translate).
- **Level 3 (Sticky Trays, Modals & Quick Baskets):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.18)`. Grounded with a warm coffee-tinted shadow rather than cool grey, maintaining color integrity across all lighting conditions.
- **Borders as Structure:** Subtle, hairline warm borders (`1px solid rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.08)`) always accompany shadows to ensure crisp card definition on low-brightness phone displays.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, accessible **Rounded** profile (`roundedness: 2` / 8px default, 16px large, 24px extra-large), projecting approachability and gentle safety.

- **Standard Containers (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Product cards, input text boxes, quantity pickers, and checkout summary tiles.
- **Category Tiles & Hero Banners (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Produce highlight modules, recipe cards, and regional cooperative campaign cards.
- **Badges, Action Chips & Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Discount indicators, "Organic" botanical seals, "In Stock" indicators, and floating Add-to-Cart controls.

## Components

### Buttons & Quick Adds
- **Primary CTA:** Tangerine Orange (`#F36B35`) fill with Coffee Brown (`#49382C`) or White text depending on contrast thresholds (minimum 4.5:1), rounded to 8px or pill-shaped, featuring a subtle bottom bevel for a pressable tactile feel.
- **Quantity Stepper:** Pill-shaped, crisp white background with warm border, containing clear Tangerine minus and plus icons surrounding a bold quantity indicator. Transitions dynamically from a standard "ADD" button upon the first touch.

### Promotional & Quality Chips
- **Deal Ribbons & Badges:** Rotated, angled pill stickers positioned at card corners. Sunshine Yellow (`#F6C945`) with brown text for offers; Coral Red (`#E95C66`) with white text for flash sales.
- **Origin & Organic Badges:** Harvest Green (`#69A94B`) pill containing subtle leaf or cooperative iconography to affirm pesticide-free certification or Sirsi farmer provenance.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) card body resting on Cream backdrop. Top zone reserved for photographic packshots or fresh harvests on neutral, uncluttered backings.
- **Footer Section:** High-contrast price locking: Strikethrough original price, bold current price in roasted brown, and bright Tangerine savings readout (`SAVE ₹45`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Crisp white interiors with `#49382C` text, surrounded by 1px warm borders (`rgba(73, 56, 44, 0.2)`). Focus state switches to a 2px ring in Tangerine Orange without layout shift.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded-sm and circular controls filled with Harvest Green when selected, signaling natural, affirmative confirmation.

### Cooperative & Financial Trust Cards
- Dedicated containers for rural credit, farmer dividend rates, and member savings cards utilizing rich cream-to-green muted gradients, warm border trims, and secure lock icons to differentiate enterprise/financial offerings from everyday FMCG goods.
