Store Visit FAQ & Accessibility

local_parking

Is parking available at the College Road Flagship?

Yes. We offer a dedicated, covered basement and surface parking lot accommodating over 40 cars and 120 two-wheelers. Security marshals are stationed to assist senior citizens and family vehicles during weekend rush hours.

payments

What payment modes are accepted at the checkout counters?

All billing counters accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), RuPay debit cards, Visa/Mastercard credit cards, Kadamba Co-operative Member Cards (with instant dividend loyalty redemptions), and cash.

assist_walker

Do you provide senior citizen and mobility assistance?

Absolutely. Dedicated attendants are stationed right at the entrance to escort elders, hold shopping baskets, or provide sanitized wheelchairs. Priority check-out Counter #1 is permanently reserved for senior shoppers and expectant mothers.

blender

Can I get whole Malenadu spices custom ground on-site?

Yes! Our Flagship store houses a traditional stone-pulverizer and cold-mill counter where you can pick whole black pepper, coriander seeds, or dry Byadagi chillies and have them fresh-ground while you finish grocery shopping.
